Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · ClipStudio by JIAN PLAY STUDIOS

Summary

ClipStudio is a video editor that works on your device. We do not collect, upload or sell your personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your device

  • Your media. Videos, photos and audio you choose are opened from your device so you can edit them. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Your projects. Your edits, project names and small preview pictures are saved in the app's own storage on your device.
  • Your settings. Your preferences and the optional display name you type in Settings.
  • Usage counts. How many projects, edits and exports you have made, shown to you on the Home screen. These numbers never leave your device.
  • Activation code. If you activate Pro, the code is saved and checked on your device. It is not linked to your identity or to a device ID.
  • Exported videos. Saved where you choose, or in Documents/ClipStudio on Android.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and there is no sign-in.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics software.
  • We do not send your media, projects or usage counts to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

ClipStudio opens only the files you pick, and saves the videos you export. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location. The Android app is built on the system web view, which declares the standard internet permission; ClipStudio does not use it to send your data, and the editor works without a connection.

Pro activation codes

ClipStudio does not take payments. If you get a Pro activation code from JIAN PLAY STUDIOS, anything you share to get it is handled outside the app, directly between you and JIAN PLAY STUDIOS.

Device backups

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include this app's saved data in your own device backup. That backup is managed by your device and your Google account, not by us.

Children

ClipStudio does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a project from the Projects screen, or use Settings → Clear all app data. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Videos you exported stay in your files until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to JIAN PLAY STUDIOS through the page or channel where you got ClipStudio.
